Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Is Quick Lead Service the service provider?

Quick Lead Service helps connect customer requests with independent local providers. The provider performs the work and confirms the price and timing directly.

How much will the service cost?

Costs depend on the provider and the work needed. Confirm the price, any inspection fee and payment terms with the provider before work starts.

Is a service guaranteed?

No. A request does not guarantee provider availability or a booking. We will only confirm a booking if the provider has agreed.

How is my request sent?

After you submit the form, WhatsApp opens with a prepared message. You choose whether to send it. The form is not stored on this website.
